Kirill Chepurin

Kirill Chepurin is a philosopher and critical theorist working across modern philosophy, literature, Romanticism, and media and technology studies. His current projects explore the concept of bliss, the relation between humanity and the cosmos, and technological images of the Earth—or, more broadly, planetary cosmograms from Romanticism to the present. His research draws on German-, English-, French-, Italian-, and Russian-language archives.
