Kirill Chepurin

Kirill Chepurin is an interdisciplinary scholar working across modern philosophy, literature, critical theory, and media and technology studies. His research explores the concept of bliss, forms of divinatory figuration, the relation between the human and the cosmic scale, and philosophical and technological images of Earth from Romanticism to the present. His work draws on German, English, French, Italian, and Russian-language archives.
