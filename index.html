Kirill Chepurin

Kirill Chepurin is an interdisciplinary scholar working across modern philosophy, literature, religion, and critical theory. His research explores the concept of bliss, forms of divinatory figuration, the relation between the human and the cosmic scale, and philosophical and technological images of the Earth from Romanticism to the present. His work draws on German, English, French, Italian, and Russian-language archives.
